Observed over expected annual-maximum exceedances of HIRDS v4 historical depths at the gauges' coordinates, water years 2017/18-2025/26 (2018/19 onward where HIRDS fitted a gauge into 2017), with 95% water-year block bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples). Expected = AEP x complete gauge-years. Grey: the in-sample control, water years up to 2014/15. 197 council gauges from seven Hilltop servers; Auckland Council's gauges were harvested but none met the inclusion rule.
