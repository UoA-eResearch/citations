Newly bare: median Sentinel-2 NDVI at least 0.5 in November-December 2022 and at most 0.3 in 15 February - 30 April 2023, with at least 2 clear observations in each window; shown as the share of valid 10 m pixels per 200 m cell, darker is more. Sites are coloured by the frozen tercile of exposure, the newly bare share of land draining to the coast within 5 km (median 0.13%, tercile cuts 0.100% and 0.147%). 63% of newly bare area lies in patches over 5 ha, mostly market-garden and plantation harvest (south and west), and 14% in slip-sized patches of 1 ha or less.
