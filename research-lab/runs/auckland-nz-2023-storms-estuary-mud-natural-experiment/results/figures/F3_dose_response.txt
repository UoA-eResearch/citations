Each point is a site with a 2023 survey (56 sites): its 2023 mud content minus its own 2010-2022 mean, against its frozen exposure. The preregistered H2 slope, with site and year fixed effects, is -0.21 pp per interquartile range of exposure (95% wild cluster bootstrap -3.06 to +3.19). The two most exposed sites (Auckland Airport and Clarks Beach, Manukau Harbour) sit beside the Pukekohe market-garden belt.
