Test-period temporal log-likelihood per target (M >= 3) event, by input magnitude cutoff, for each AVN partition. Standard ETAS (published, grey dashed) and the neural point process (published, orange) are Stockman et al.'s released results; the refit standard ETAS (light grey dotted), ETAS-I with rate-dependent detection (A, dark blue) and time-varying completeness (B, light blue) are fitted here on the same training events. The preregistered configurations are the lowest cutoffs (1.2, 1.2, 1.3). Model B's fit at Visso 3.0 is degenerate (off scale).
