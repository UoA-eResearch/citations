For each published weekly JADE run, the simulated distribution of the average wholesale price at Haywards in the run's first fully out-of-sample week (stage 2), against the price actually settled in that week. The band spans the 10th to 90th percentile of the run's 91 historical inflow sequences. Orange markers are weeks when the realised price finished above the simulated 90th percentile, which a calibrated model would do about a tenth of the time; here it happens in 90 of 152 weeks. Log scale.
