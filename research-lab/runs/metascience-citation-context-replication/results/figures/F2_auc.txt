F2. How well the share of negative citing papers predicts whether a finding later failed to replicate (area under the ROC curve, 95% bootstrap intervals). Blue: citing papers published before the first replication, for the 1,075 originals with at least 5 such citing papers with context (the hypothesis). Orange: citing papers published at least a year after it, for the 851 of those originals with at least 5 such papers: the positive control, which a detector that can see replication language should pass.
