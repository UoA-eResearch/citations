Exploratory: the share of an original's citing papers whose citation context uses explicit replication-failure language (keyword detector K1), by the citing paper's year relative to the original's first replication, averaged over originals (each original with at least 3 citing papers with context that year counts equally; replication papers themselves excluded). In the six years before the first recorded replication, findings that went on to fail already draw such language at a somewhat higher, roughly flat rate (0.24-0.55% of citing papers, against 0.01-0.26%), probably echoing earlier attempts; from the replication year on the rate is three to four times higher (1.1-1.8%).
