Calibration of the pre-update probability of being best of the top-ranked treatment against the share of networks in which that treatment is still top-ranked, among the treatments already compared, once the withheld studies are added back (a newly added treatment that ranks first is not counted). Bins of width 0.2 with at least 3 networks; bars are 95% Wilson intervals. Points above the diagonal mean the stated probability understates how often the top rank holds.
