**Text 1**

Let $a$ and $b$ be points of $R^k$. Determine a point $c \in R^k$ and a radius $r > 0$ for which the condition $|x-a|=2|x-b|$ holds exactly when $| x - c | = r$. Show that these satisfy $3c = 4b - a$ and $3r = 2 |b - a|$.

**Text 2**
