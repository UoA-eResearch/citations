For each PCBA-2026 target, the Kendall τ between its ranking of the five methods by EF1% and the consensus ranking on LIT-PCBA as published (all 28 targets). Orange: the mean τ between a ranking computed on half of a target's evaluation set and the LIT consensus; dark: the mean τ between the two halves of the same target (5 random halvings per target; post hoc, results/tables/posthoc_review.json). Halving barely moves the agreement with LIT, while two halves of one target agree with each other far more.
