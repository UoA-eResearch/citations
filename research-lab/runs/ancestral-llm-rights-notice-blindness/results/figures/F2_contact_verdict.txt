F2. Post hoc (deviations D6). In the agent framing, models fill a field "permission_or_contact_required" (yes / no / unclear). A conventional request to contact the providers, or a data use agreement, becomes "yes" every time in every model. The Local Contexts elements mostly become "unclear": the BC and TK Notices, whose texts say permissions "may need" to be sought, and TK Open to Collaboration, whose text expresses a community's openness to collaboration and asks nothing of the user. TK Community Use Only is "yes" 28% of the time (Qwen3 72%). 663 responses per condition (221 records x 3 models), 50 unparseable responses excluded.
