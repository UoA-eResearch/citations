Each point is one of the 24 grid-exit units (networks sharing grid exit points merged): the change between the first and last summers in mean net grid offtake per ICP against the change in installed solar per ICP (all distribution-connected solar, including farms). The two units with the largest solar growth per ICP are labelled; both added solar farms. Evening offtake fell with solar too, so the right-hand panel nets it out. The estimates in the report use all twelve summers with unit and summer fixed effects and temperature; this figure is descriptive.
