Difference-in-differences: the change in plug-in registrations from January-March to May-October, minus the same change for non-plug hybrids, which were not moved to road user charges. April is excluded as a transition month. The 2024 estimate carries a block-bootstrap 95% interval; the placebo years use the identical windows; those dates are not policy-free (1 April 2022 began the full Clean Car Discount, and rebate cuts took effect on 1 July 2023).
