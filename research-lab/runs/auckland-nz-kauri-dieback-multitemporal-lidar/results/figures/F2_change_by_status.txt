Mean change (95% block bootstrap) in the 95th percentile of canopy height within 15 m of each surveyed tree, after masking cells whose ground height changed by more than 1 m. Random forest points: 1,000 points with 2016-18 canopy above 15 m, at least 50 m from any survey record; their mean rise of 1.25 m includes any systematic difference between the two LiDAR surveys. The 2014-15 trees stand by tracks (median 5 m away); the 2021 trees stand deeper in the forest.
