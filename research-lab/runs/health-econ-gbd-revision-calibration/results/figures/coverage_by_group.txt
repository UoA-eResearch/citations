Coverage of GBD 2023 estimates by GBD 2021 95% uncertainty intervals, 137 level-3 causes × 2019 and 2021, by WHO vital-registration quality group. Bars are 95% two-way (country × cause) bootstrap intervals. Both measures sit far below what calibrated intervals would give and, counting every cell equally, hardly differ by data quality. Grey squares weight cells by GBD 2021 deaths (review-requested, exploratory): the low group then falls to 70%, about two-thirds of the gap coming from large revisions to major causes in China and India.
