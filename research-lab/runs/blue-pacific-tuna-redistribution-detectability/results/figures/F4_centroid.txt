Trend in the mean longitude of skipjack within the domain. The top two rows use every cell fished that year, so they move east whenever the fleet opens new eastern grounds. The remaining rows hold the cells fixed - those fished in at least the stated share of the 42 years - and use only years in which at least 80% of them were actually fished. Beside each observed trend (orange, 95% block-bootstrap interval) is SEAPODYM's projected 2011-2050 trend computed on the same cells (yellow diamond). The narrow western footprints give a clean null but register only a fraction of the projected shift, so they cannot tell no shift from the projected one; the wider footprints, where the projected shift is visible, give trends that depend on the threshold. Full threshold-by-coverage grid in results/tables/footprint_grid.json (deviations D8).
