Trend in the mean longitude of skipjack within the domain. The top two rows use every cell fished that year, weighted by observed catch and by observed density (catch per fishing day times cell area); the next two restrict density to a fixed set of 86 core cells (141.5-159.5E) fished in at least 80% of years, first over all years and then over the 31 years in which those cells were actually covered (deviations D6, D7). Each is ENSO-adjusted with a 95% block-bootstrap interval. The SEAPODYM rows use modelled biomass; the projected value is the four-ESM mean linear trend for 2011-2050 and has no interval. A centroid over fished cells moves east whenever the fishery opens new eastern grounds, so only the fixed-footprint rows speak to where the fish are.
