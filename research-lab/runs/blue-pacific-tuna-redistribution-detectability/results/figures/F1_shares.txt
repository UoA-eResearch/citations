Share of skipjack inside the twelve EEZ areas of the ten Pacific SIDS in Bell et al. (2021), within the domain 20S-20N, 120E-228E, each shown as percentage points from its own 1980-2009 mean so that observed catch shares (about 81% before 2010) and modelled biomass shares (about 49%) share one axis. Solid lines are WCPFC public-domain purse-seine data: catch share (the preregistered primary series), density share (each fished cell weighted by catch per fishing day; still a share over the cells fished that year, so not free of the fleet's footprint), and the share of fishing days. The dashed line is SEAPODYM skipjack biomass: the reanalysis-forced historical run to 2010, then the mean of the four ESM-forced RCP 8.5 projections. The dotted line marks the 2010 closure of the high-seas pockets to purse seiners.
