Injection-recovery power for a linear decline in the primary series (EEZ catch share, 1980-2021): the fitted trend is removed, a known decline added, block-resampled residuals re-added, and the same ENSO-adjusted estimator re-fitted. The preregistered one-sided HAC test fires in 14% of simulations with no decline injected, against a nominal 5%, so its curve overstates power; the size-calibrated curve takes its critical value from those null simulations. The vertical line is SEAPODYM's projected 2011-2050 rate of change in the identical share (0.117 pp/yr), the dotted line 80% power.
