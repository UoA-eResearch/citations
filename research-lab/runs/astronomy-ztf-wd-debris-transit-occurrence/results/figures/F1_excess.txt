Per-star faint-outlier excess pooled over ZTF g and r (good epochs only), for 108,493 sample white dwarfs with at least 100 good epochs. Vertical lines mark the 18 known debris-transit white dwarfs in the sample (blue: flagged by either preregistered rule; orange: missed). The short-dip rule also needs a binomial p < 1e-6 and faint epochs on at least 3 nights; the long-dip rule uses 30-day bins and is not shown here.
