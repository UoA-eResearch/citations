Each point is a retained, non-engineered islet of at least 1 ha with a Sentinel-2 rate (150 islets in 6 atolls). (a) Theil-Sen rate of uncorrected area against the rate after removing each islet's tide response; the dashed line is equality. The mean absolute difference is 2.08%/yr (H2, preregistered); with water levels permuted within each islet it is 0.29%/yr (post hoc noise floor). (b) The difference against the islet's tide slope (% of area per metre) times the Theil-Sen trend of the gauge water level at the image times; the sampled water level drifts by tens of millimetres a year at some atolls over the decade, and the bias follows. 4 islets lie outside the axes of (a).
