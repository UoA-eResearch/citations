F1. What the method can detect. Simulated ports with a known complete closure, run through exactly the preregistered algorithm (120 replicates per point, dispersion 0.25; the full grid, including dispersion 1, is in results/tables/detectability.json). The value at 0 days is the simulated false-alarm rate. The simulation draws independent noise; real port series vary more serially, and at storm-free dates the real pipeline flags 10% of runs, rising to 15% above two calls a day (deviations D8). Of the 62 Pacific island ports, 50 average under 0.5 calls a day and 7 more between 0.5 and 1.
