The sign count here hides a turn in volume: 2P ultimate recoverable was revised up by 227-305 PJ a year between the 2018 and 2021 editions and down every year from 2022 (see the report's PJ table). Among field-years whose ultimate recoverable gas volume changed by more than 1% between consecutive vintages, the share revised downward, with Wilson 95% intervals. A calibrated proved (1P) estimate should rarely be revised down (dashed line at 10%); a calibrated proved-plus-probable (2P) estimate should be revised down about half the time (solid line).
