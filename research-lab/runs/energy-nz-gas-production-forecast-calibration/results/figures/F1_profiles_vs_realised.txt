Coloured lines: the national total of the field-by-field gas production profile that operators reported to MBIE in each vintage of the petroleum reserves workbook (the vintage is the 'as at 1 January' date inside the workbook). Black: realised net production, from MBIE's energy overview, which reproduces the sum of the published field-level net production and whose 2025 value matches MBIE's published 102 PJ. Profiles run 14 years ahead; the axis is cut at 2032.
