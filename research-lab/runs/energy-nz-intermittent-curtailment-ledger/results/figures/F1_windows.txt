Curtailed energy as a share of available (bias-corrected potential) energy for New Zealand's offered wind and solar plants, by 12-month window (the first window is January 2020 to June 2021). The full bar is the preregistered measure. Its grey part includes the mass that the rule flags by construction in unconstrained periods (about 5% of them), which is forecast error rather than curtailment. The blue part is curtailment in periods whose node price was at or below the plant's own offer price.
