Grey: contrasts by the number of DE genes the full-size comparison reports, using every donor, as a practitioner would run it (median 100 donors). Orange: how many of those also exceed their own matched healthy-vs-healthy null (BH q < 0.05). The percentage above each bar is the share that does. The two are not the same comparison: the null test runs at matched group sizes (median 13 against 13), because a null needs two disjoint healthy groups, so part of the gap is donor count rather than signal.
