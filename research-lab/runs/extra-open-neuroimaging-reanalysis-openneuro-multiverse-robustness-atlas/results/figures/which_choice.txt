How much each choice moves the thresholded map. For each dataset, the mean signed Dice between configurations that differ only in that choice (the first four, all datasets with R), then, on the 18 datasets where both are defined, nilearn maps that differ only in confounds on the FSL subjects (up to 20) and FSL against nilearn maps that differ only in software. Lower means the choice matters more. Smoothing and confounds move the maps most; switching software moves them less than switching confounds (mean 0.77 against 0.65; software higher in 16 of 18). Software bundles smoothing kernel, HRF shape, high-pass filter and scaling.
