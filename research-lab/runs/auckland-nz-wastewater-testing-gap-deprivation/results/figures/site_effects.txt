Each point is one of the 57 catchments: its mean ascertainment index (log reported cases per 100,000 minus log wastewater copies per person per day, after removing the national week-to-week pattern) against its population-weighted NZDep2023 score. Point size reflects the number of weeks. The line is the unadjusted fit; the preregistered estimate (ratio 0.86 per SD) comes only after adjusting for sampler, population, age and region.
