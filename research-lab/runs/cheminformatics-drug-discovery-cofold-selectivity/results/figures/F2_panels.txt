The panel as measured and as predicted, ligands in rows (ordered by mean measured pKd) and kinases in columns (likewise). The lower row removes each ligand's own mean, which is what the selectivity question is about: horizontal banding in the lower-right panel would mean the model varies by ligand but not by target, while agreement between the two lower panels would mean it tracks the real selectivity profile. Each row shares one colour scale, so the paler lower-right panel shows directly how much smaller the predicted differences between kinases are.
