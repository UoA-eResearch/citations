## New issues

1. **Minor, substantive: the peS2o registry still dates documents by Crossref self-updates.** `pes2o.registry()`
   takes any Crossref `update-to` of type retraction, the record's own included. 166 of the 17,416 matched documents
   (1.0%; 132 s2orc, 34 s2ag; 107 with prefix 10.1016) are dated only this way, and their "retraction" years are issue
   years (29 in 2020, 54 in 2021, 16 in 2022). D4 established that these are not retraction dates and D5 moved the
   PMC arm's 412 to the undated stratum on that ground, yet the same kind of document is counted as "retracted on or
   before 2022-07-07" in the peS2o arm; some were probably retracted after peS2o's 2023-01-03 cutoff and could not
   have carried a marker. In the 3,235 overlap, 111 documents carry the PMC cohort's self-update date source (103 by
   my own derivation from the raw Crossref file): PMC own-marker 82.5%, peS2o 8.7%. Effect: whole set 47.2% → 47.6%
   without them; overlap 95.7% / 9.1% → 96.2% / 9.1% (n 3,132). No conclusion moves, but the report should say so in
   one sentence (§peS2o and D5), or exclude the 166 for consistency with D5.
2. **Minor, text against code.** `deviations.md:80–81` says the downstream subset is restricted to "the primary or
   recent stratum"; `pes2o_secondary.py` applies no stratum filter and 2 of the 3,235 are in the undated stratum
   (3,233 otherwise: 95.8% / 9.1%). Align the wording with the code.
3. **Minor, cohort label.** `report.md:100` ("moves the D3 unmarked share from 2.77% to 2.79%") is a
   preregistered-cohort figure in a section that precedes the "D5 cohort unless stated" note at line 126; the D5
   cohort's D3 share is 2.16%. Add "(preregistered cohort)". Everything else I checked carries its label.
4. **Minor, wording: three overstatements.**
   - `report.md:28` "the 412 undatable Elsevier documents": 354 of 412 carry Elsevier's DOI prefix (175 named
     Elsevier, 120 of the 138 publisher-unknown are 10.1016); 58 are Springer (21), Nature (16), BMC (10) and
     others. Say "mostly Elsevier", as line 170 does.
   - `report.md:55–56` "most of them from Elsevier and Cell Press journals": 191 of the 382 (Cell Press 139,
     Elsevier 43, nine more by DOI prefix), exactly half. Say "about half".
   - `report.md:33` "It comes almost entirely from IEEE's 2009–2011 retractions": IEEE accounts for 6,179 of the
     8,225 marked documents (75%); without IEEE's 6,625 documents the marked share is 19.0% (23.3% of
     title-and-abstract records). Say "three-quarters of the marked documents are IEEE's; without IEEE the share is
     19%", which also makes the bullet's "high only because of IEEE" precise.
