Retraction Watch retractions 2000-2021 and works that cited them before the retraction with no shared author or institution (depth 1), or cited such a work (depth 2; up to 20 per retraction). Each line is the retraction-weighted mean of exposed minus matched-control log(1 + citations) by year, relative to the year before the retraction. Grey (preregistered): controls from the same source and year citing no retracted work, matched on citations to T-2; exposed works rise faster before T. Orange (post hoc A): the same controls matched on the trajectory to T-1; exposed works jump at T. Blue (post hoc B): controls that also cite retracted work, but only work retracted after T+4; no divergence.
